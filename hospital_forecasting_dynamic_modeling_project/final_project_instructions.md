Final Project 
• Project Goal: The objective of this final project is to apply time series and/or dynamic 
models to a real-world dataset and generate meaningful predictions. 
• Topic Selection: You are responsible for choosing your project topic. Select one based on 
your personal interests, career goals, or relevant work experience. 
• Data Requirement: You may use any dataset, and larger datasets are encouraged  
o The only requirement is that the dataset must include a time-related variable—such as 
year, month, day, or intra-day frequency. 
o If you're having trouble finding data, consider the following sources (additional links are 
provided on the next page):  
▪ https://www.kaggle.com/datasets 
▪ https://github.com/awesomedata/awesome-public-datasets 
• You cannot submit a project that has already been completed or submitted for another class. 
• Group project is allowed. Each participant will share the same grade.   
Three Components of the Final Project 
(1) PowerPoint Presentation (Optional for Undergraduate Credit) 
• Prepare a slide deck (maximum 10 slides) and a 10-minute presentation. There are two 
options of presentation. 
o Live presentation on the final instruction day. Sign-up is required as only 10 slots are 
available. A participation bonus will be awarded for real-time presentations. 
o Pre-recorded presentation using Zoom or YouTube. Submit the recording along with your 
project materials. 
(2) Written Report 
• Your written report should include the following elements:  
o  Project purpose and motivation: Explain the objective and significance of your analysis. 
o  Visualizations: Present clear, high-quality graphs and charts. 
o  Model selection and justification: Describe the models you used and explain why you 
chose them. Include test-set or out-of-sample validation.  
o  Narrative and insights: Tell a compelling story and summarize your key findings and 
takeaways. 
• Length: 5–20 pages (including charts and figures).  
(3) Codes: R Script, R Markdown, or Python Jupyter Notebook 
• Submission Details: Submit all project materials (slides, report, code, and dataset) via 
Canvas by midnight, December 18.
