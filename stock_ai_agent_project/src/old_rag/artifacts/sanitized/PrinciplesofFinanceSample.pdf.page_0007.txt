Levels versus Percentage Changes The same information can be presented in graphs several different ways. The particular format you choose will depend on how you are using the data. Figure 3.19 shows the real GDP of Japan for 2010-2020. This chart is created showing the level of real GDP. The steep drop in 2020 highlights the economic decline associated with the COVID-19 pandemic. Looking at the chart, it is easy to see that after 10 years of a general upward trend, Japan's GDP quickly fell to a level not seen in the previous decade as COVID-19 began spreading in early 2020. Figure 3.19 Real Gross Domestic Product for Japan, 2010-202013 The vertical axis in Figure 3.19 is measured in yen. Over the time period shown, the real GDP ranged from 500 trillion yen to 560 trillion yen. The general trend (until COVID-19) was upward, indicating growth in the Japanese economy.
